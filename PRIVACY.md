# Privacy Policy for Xandyr's Turbo Rush

_Last updated: October 22, 2026_

Xandyr's Turbo Rush ("the app") is a racing game for young children, made by UnFiltered Ink.
We built it so that it collects nothing about you or your child.

## What the app collects

**Nothing.** The app does not collect, store, send or share any personal information. There are:

- no accounts or sign-ins
- no ads
- no analytics, tracking or crash reporting
- no chat, camera, microphone, location or contacts access
- no links out of the app to websites or social media

Game progress (level, coins, upgrades, the car and track your child picked) and the time the free trial
started are saved **only on the device**, inside the app's own storage. If Android backup is turned on, Android
may include that small amount of game data in the device's own Google backup so it survives a reinstall.
We never see it.

## Purchases

The optional **Turbo Pass** subscription is sold and handled entirely by **Google Play**. Google processes the
payment and tells the app only whether a subscription is active. We never receive payment details, names or
email addresses. Before Google Play's purchase screen opens, the app asks a grown-ups-only math question.

Google's handling of purchase information is covered by the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can cancel the subscription at any time in the
Google Play Store under **Payments & subscriptions**.

## Children

The app is designed for children and follows the Google Play Families policy and the U.S. Children's Online
Privacy Protection Act (COPPA): it collects no personal information from children.
